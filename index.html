THE BEGINNING

Who Was Rani Gaidinliu?

Rani Gaidinliu was a Naga spiritual and political leader who became one of the young voices of resistance against British rule in Northeast India.

Born in 1915, her journey began at a remarkably young age and would eventually make her one of the notable figures of India's struggle against colonial rule.

1915

The World She Was Born Into

Rani Gaidinliu was born on 26 January 1915 in present-day Manipur, into the Rongmei Naga community. She grew up in a time when British colonial rule extended deep into the northeastern regions of India.

The lives of her people were increasingly affected by foreign control, new systems of governance, and cultural interference. It was a world where identity, belief, and freedom were all being challenged.

AGE 13

The Beginning of Her Resistance

At the age of thirteen, Rani Gaidinliu joined the Heraka movement, a spiritual and social movement among the Zeliangrong Naga people. Her involvement marked the beginning of a journey that would place her at the centre of resistance to British rule.

AGE 16

The Price of Resistance

In 1932, when she was only sixteen, Rani Gaidinliu was arrested by the British authorities. She was sentenced to life imprisonment and spent years in captivity.
